Short a goalkeeper? Need a team to play? Post it in thirty seconds and settle it on WhatsApp. No sign-up, no fees, no ads.

- Two boards: GK Lagbe for games short a goalkeeper, Opponent Lagbe for teams looking for a match
- No accounts: each post is controlled by a private manage link
- Browser and Telegram alerts when a game nearby needs a keeper
- Phone numbers are never listed on the board
